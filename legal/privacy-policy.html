Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Zero Tracking & Client-Side Execution

This website and all included digital security tools operate strictly on client-side logic within your web browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal inputs, passwords, files, or IP analysis data to external database servers.

2. Third-Party Data APIs

Certain network analysis tools (e.g. IP Geolocation, HaveIBeenPwned k-Anonymity checks, DNS Over HTTPS) query public APIs directly from your browser. We utilize zero-knowledge techniques such as k-Anonymity SHA-1 prefixing to ensure full privacy.

3. Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage solely to remember your UI preference (Light Mode or Dark Mode).
